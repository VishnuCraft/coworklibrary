Must have

The five an owner pays for

Every owner we simulated said the hard part is chasing fees, not finding students.

01Must

Every seat belongs to a real student

BeforeSeat flips to "Occupied". Name typed in a note.
NowFull record: phone, parent, exam, shift, fee, paid-till.
Fees, reminders and parent calls all need a person on the seat.
02Must

One seat, three shifts, three prices

BeforeSeat is just free or taken. Every shift costs ₹500.
NowMorning and evening students share a seat. Each shift has its own price.
Full day blocks bothMorningEvening
Selling one seat twice is how libraries make money.
03Must

Fees: who owes, how much, since when

BeforeNot in the product.
NowPending total, due list, mark paid (1–6 months, cash/UPI), monthly collection.
The #1 pain in all 50 owner conversations.
04Must

WhatsApp reminder in one tap

BeforeWhatsApp only alerted the owner about new requests.
NowReady message to student or parent: name, amount, due date, UPI ID.
Owners already chase on WhatsApp by hand. A message to the parent gets the fee paid.
05Must

A seat map the owner reads at a glance

BeforeSeat Inventory: a 60-row list over 3 pages.
NowHall map by shift. Tap a seat to open the student.
Due in 2–3 daysTomorrowDue / overdue
Owners think in their hall. Red seats are the fastest fee report.
Should have · Polish

Six smaller fixes

These join the student side to the owner side and clean up the copy.

06Should

Approving a request creates the student

BeforeApprove, then retype everything
NowPick seat and fee, done
Owner calls, says yes, moves on.
07Should

A listing students can compare

BeforeName, address, filler text
NowTimings, prices, AC, facilities
Students choose on price, distance and AC.
08Should

Builder that fits and protects

BeforeRow cut off at 8 seats
NowFits; occupied seats locked
Never lose a paying student's seat.
09Should

Libraries only

BeforeCoworking, hostels, flats, bus. The only listing was a Mumbai meeting room.
NowStudy libraries, city and AC filters
Win one city's libraries first.
10Polish

Plain words, no fake badges

Before"Space portfolio telemetry", Laravel in footer, "Verified Host"
Now"Free seats", "Fees pending", no badge
Owners aren't tech people. Fake trust breaks trust.
11Polish

Phone-first owner menu

BeforeDesktop-first sidebar
NowBottom tabs with request count
Owners run the desk from their phone.
